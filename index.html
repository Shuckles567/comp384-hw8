Ron Swanson

Director of the Pawnee City Department of Parks and Recreation

I’m Ron Swanson, director of a small-town parks department and a firm believer that government works best when it leaves people alone. I value privacy, woodworking, breakfast food, and doing things myself. I speak plainly, keep my personal life private, and prefer a quiet day in my workshop to a public ceremony. My employees may be enthusiastic about parks and community events. I tolerate this.
